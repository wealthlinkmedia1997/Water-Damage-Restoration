Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

WealthLink Media (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when a water damage restoration owner submits the form on this site.

Information we collect

The form asks for your business name, full name, and phone number. If a Google Places key is configured, choosing a suggestion can also store a place id and the address Google returns. If a future version of the form asks about revenue or a Google Business Profile, those answers are collected too.

How we use it

We use this information to call you within 5 minutes, check whether your 30-mile radius is still open, and decide whether the Match Method is a fit. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Where it goes

Until a webhook address is set in the site config, the form does not send your answers to another server. It stores them in this browser for the thank-you page, then moves you forward. When a webhook is set, the same answers are posted to that address so the lead can be worked. Calendar pages stay empty until a booking link is configured.

Calls and texts

Sending your number is consent for WealthLink Media, including Andre Slyter, to call or text that number about this application. Message rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts.

Advertising pixels

A Meta pixel loads only when a pixel id is present in the site config. While that value is blank, this site does not load the pixel.

Your requests

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the details you submitted by contacting Andre Slyter through the form on this site. A public email and phone are not listed here.
